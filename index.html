Hi! I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at the University of California, Berkeley, where I'm working with Dr. Chiwei Yan and Dr. Manxi Wu.

I received my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from the Department of Industrial Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2026, where I was advised by Dr. Hai Jiang. I received my B.Eng. in Traffic Engineering from the Department of Civil Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2021.

My research focuses on pricing, dynamic stochastic matching, and data-driven optimization, with applications to on-demand transportation and logistics platforms, such as ridesharing and online food delivery.
