I am an incoming Postdoctoral Scholar at the University of California, Berkeley, where I will work with Dr. Chiwei Yan and Dr. Manxi Wu.

I received my Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from the Department of Industrial Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2026, where I was advised by Dr. Hai Jiang. I received my B.Eng. in Traffic Engineering from the Department of Civil Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2021.

My research focuses on pricing, dynamic stochastic matching, discrete choice modeling, and data-driven optimization, with applications to on-demand transportation and logistics platforms, such as ride-hailing, ride-pooling, and online food delivery.
